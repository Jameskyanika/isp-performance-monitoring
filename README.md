# ISP Performance Monitoring and Optimization

This repository documents my practical work in enterprise internet-service monitoring, bandwidth-utilization analysis, performance testing, capacity planning and technical reporting.

All reports and screenshots published here are sanitized to protect company, customer and ISP information.

## Project Overview

The purpose of this project was to monitor a dedicated enterprise internet connection and determine:

- Whether the ISP was delivering the subscribed bandwidth
- Whether the connection was stable
- Whether traffic was approaching saturation
- How much bandwidth remained available for peak demand
- Whether an upgrade or optimization was required
- Whether latency and packet loss were affecting business operations

## ISP Capacity-Utilization Analysis

Cacti traffic graphs were reviewed across several monitoring periods, including:

- 1 hour
- 2 hours
- 6 hours
- 12 hours
- 1 day
- 2 days
- 4 days
- 1 week
- weeks
- Months
- Years

The 100 Mbps connection was generally utilized at approximately 50–60 Mbps.

The analysis found:

- Stable and predictable traffic patterns
- No sustained bandwidth saturation
- Short traffic spikes within acceptable operating limits
- Approximately 40–50 Mbps of remaining bandwidth capacity
- Sufficient capacity for temporary increases in business activity

## Weekly Performance Testing

Ookla speed tests were conducted daily

### Average Results

- **Average download:** 93.92 Mbps
- **Average upload:** 85.43 Mbps
- **Average latency:** 3.3 ms

The tests showed that the ISP consistently delivered speeds close to the subscribed 100 Mbps bandwidth with generally low latency.
